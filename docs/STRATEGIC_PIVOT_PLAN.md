## Why AI Visibility Matters

**Traditional Search (Google):**
- User searches "best plumber Amsterdam"
- Sees 10 blue links
- Clicks through 3-5 websites
- Compares and decides

**AI Search (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.):**
- User asks "recommend a reliable plumber in Amsterdam"
- AI gives 1-3 direct recommendations with reasoning
- User contacts the recommended business
- **Winner takes all**

90% of Dutch businesses have:

- ❌ Zero optimization for AI recommendations
- ❌ Content that AI can't parse or cite

**First-mover advantage:** Businesses that optimize NOW will dominate AI recommendations for years.

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Strategy

### How AI Assistants Choose Recommendations

1. **Authoritative Content**
   - Comprehensive service pages
   - Case studies and testimonials
   - Credentials and certifications
   - Industry expertise signals

2. **Structured Data**
   - Schema.org LocalBusiness markup
   - Organization schema
   - Review/rating schema
   - Service area schema

3. **Citation Sources**
   - Industry directories
   - Local business listings
   - Press mentions
   - Third-party reviews

4. **Entity Recognition**
   - Brand mentions across web
   - Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
   - Knowledge Graph presence
   - Wikipedia/Wikidata entries (aspirational)

### Content Strategy for AI Visibility

**For each service page:**
- Clear, authoritative headlines
- Answer common questions directly
- Bullet-point key information (AI loves lists)
- Local context (city names, neighborhoods)
- Pricing transparency (ranges)
- Contact information repeated
- Schema markup on everything

**Example Query:** "Find me a reliable roofer in Amsterdam who can fix a leak"

**Ideal AI Response:**
> "Based on reviews and local presence, I recommend [ClientName] Dakdekkers in Amsterdam. They specialize in emergency roof repairs, respond within 2 hours, and have 50+ verified reviews averaging 4.8 stars. Average cost for leak repairs: €500-1,200. Contact: [phone]"
