## 🎨 Design System Enhancements

### Colors
- Primary: `#D62D3A` (REBEL Red)
- Gradient: `from-[#D62D3A] via-[#E63946] to-[#C72030]`
- Background: `#FFFBF6` (Cream)
- Text: `#595658` (Gray), `#202020` (Dark)

### Typography
- Font: Poppins (300-900 weights)
- Headings: 56px-64px (Hero), 48px-56px (Sections)
- Body: 18px-20px with relaxed leading
- All text optimized for readability

### Animations
- **Custom keyframes**: float (6s), glow (3s), pulse
- **Transitions**: 300ms smooth on all interactions
- **Hover effects**: scale, translate, shadow
- **Scroll behavior**: Smooth page-wide

### Components
- **Glass effects**: `bg-white/10 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/20`
- **Shadow elegant**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 60px -15px rgba(214, 45, 58, 0.3)`
- **Hover lift**: `-translate-y-2` with shadow
- **Custom scrollbar**: REBEL red with cream track
